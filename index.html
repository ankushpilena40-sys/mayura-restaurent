The Mayura Story

Where the warmth of Kerala
meets Culver City.

Mayura Indian Kitchen began with a love for the traditional recipes, vibrant spices and comforting meals of Kerala and South India. Since opening in 2006, our family-owned restaurant has grown into a gathering place for longtime guests and first-time visitors alike.

Mayura means "peacock" in Sanskrit — a symbol of grace, beauty and tradition. Those qualities continue to inspire everything we do, from the colors on each plate to the way every guest is welcomed through our doors.

Our vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes are prepared in separate kitchens, with vegan and gluten-free options available on request.

Signature Flavors

Start with something delicious.

Dosas & South Indian Classics

Golden, fermented rice-and-lentil crepes served with chutneys and sambar — plain, masala, paper-thin and Mysore-style.

Kerala Curries & Seafood

Coconut milk, curry leaves and roasted spice — Malabar coconut roasts, Kottayam red curry and fish pollichathu.

Thalassery Biryani

Aromatic jeerakasala rice layered with chicken, mutton, shrimp or vegetables in traditional Malabar style.

Vegetarian Favorites

Palak paneer, chana masala, Kerala avial and more — a full vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free spread.
